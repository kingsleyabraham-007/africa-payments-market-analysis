# 🌍 Africa Payments Market Analysis

## Overview

This project examines the growth of digital payments across African markets and identifies opportunities for fintech companies seeking to expand their payment products and services.

## 🎯 Business Question

**Which factors make an African market attractive for digital payment expansion, and where should a fintech prioritize its growth efforts?**

## 🔎 Areas of Analysis

* Digital payment adoption
* Mobile money and digital wallets
* Cross-border payments
* Merchant acceptance
* Consumer payment behavior
* Payment infrastructure
* Market competition
* Partnership opportunities
* Market-entry considerations

## 🧠 Analytical Framework

The project evaluates markets using four major dimensions:

**Market Attractiveness**
Market size, digital adoption and growth potential.

**Customer Need**
Existing payment problems and underserved customer segments.

**Competitive Opportunity**
Existing providers, product gaps and differentiation opportunities.

**Execution Feasibility**
Infrastructure, partnerships, operational requirements and regulatory considerations.

## 📊 Methodology

1. Define the business question.
2. Collect publicly available market data.
3. Clean and organize the data.
4. Compare selected African markets.
5. Identify patterns and market gaps.
6. Develop commercial recommendations.

## 💼 Business Applications

The findings can support decisions around:

* Market expansion
* Merchant acquisition
* Partnership development
* Product localization
* Customer acquisition
* Cross-border payment opportunities
